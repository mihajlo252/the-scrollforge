## 08-10-2026 · Campaigns

# New

**Campaigns** — Game Masters can now run campaigns. Open **Campaigns** from the account menu to forge one, pick its system (D&D 5e or Daggerheart, which can't be changed later) and give it a pitch for players to read.

**Campaign Features** 

- **Campaign glyphs** — choose your campaign's sigil from a gallery of glyphs when you forge it.
- **Join requests** — players browse open campaigns, search and filter them by system, and present their characters to the GM. Players can bring more than one hero to the same campaign, and only characters of the campaign's system can join.
- **Running your table** — the GM accepts or rejects requests, sees the whole party at a glance, and can remove characters from the campaign.
- **Read-only character sheets** — the GM can open any party member's sheet to look over it. Nothing can be changed except adding items to that character's inventory.
- **Delete a campaign** — the GM can delete a campaign from its card or its page by typing the campaign's name to confirm.
- **Rearrange campaigns** — drag and drop campaign cards, and the GM can reorder the party, just like characters, traits and other cards.

**Changes**

- **Account menu** — The account menu now opens directly beneath its button.
- **Daggerheart character sheet PDF** — your Background and Connections questions and answers are now written onto the downloaded sheet.

**Fixes**

- Popups no longer flicker when they open or close.
- Pressing **Escape** in a popup opened from another popup now closes only the top one.
- The character filter on the Profile screen scrolls sideways instead of overflowing on narrow screens.
- Assorted layout fixes across character creation and the character sheets.
