# Campaign acts

Acts are where a Game Master plans the story of a campaign before the session: what happens, the choices the party might face, and who they'll fight. Players only see the parts you reveal.

## Writing acts

Open a campaign you run and scroll to **The Story** under the party.

- **New Act** — give it a title and an optional summary. The new act opens straight away.
- **Reorder** — drag an act card by its grip. Acts are numbered in this order (Act I, Act II…).
- **Edit or delete** — use the buttons at the top of the act's page.

## Modules

Fill an act from the **Add to this act** tiles at the bottom of its page:

- **Story** — narration, scenes and lore, written as you'd read them at the table.
- **Option** — a choice the party faces. Add a path for each thing you expect them to try, with notes, and pick the act each path **leads to**. On the act page, select the link to jump to that act.
- **Adversary** — a stat block for a foe. Daggerheart campaigns get tier, type, difficulty, thresholds, HP, stress, attack and features. D&D campaigns get AC, HP, speed, ability scores, challenge, traits and actions. To reuse a foe from another act, pick it under **Copy from the campaign pool**: its whole stat block is filled in, and changing the copy doesn't change the original.
- **Encounter** — a scene with its setting and objective, and the adversaries in it. Pick adversaries from any act's Adversary modules and set how many of each.
- **Environment** — a place. Daggerheart campaigns get tier, type, difficulty, impulses and features. D&D campaigns get terrain, hazards with DCs, and features. Either can list the adversaries that might turn up there.
- **Event** — something that happens: its trigger, what happens, and how it might turn out. Turn on **Use a countdown** to tick it down during play.

Every module can be edited, deleted, collapsed, and dragged into a new order.

## Running an encounter

Every adversary in an encounter gets its own tracker: an encounter with 5× Bandit shows Bandit 1 to Bandit 5.

- **Daggerheart** — HP starts full (e.g. 10/10) and Stress starts empty (e.g. 0/6). Select a slot to set HP or Stress to it, and select the last filled slot again to take one away.
- **D&D** — use **−** and **+** to change hit points. Hold a button to keep counting.
- An adversary whose HP runs out is shown as **Defeated**.
- **Reset marks** clears every tracker in the encounter.
- Open **Stat block** under an adversary's name to check its stats without leaving the encounter.

Marks and countdown ticks are saved automatically, so they're still there after a reload.

## Revealing the story to players

Everything starts hidden.

- **Reveal an act** — select **Hidden from players** at the top of the act. Seated players then see its title and summary under **The Story** on the campaign page.
- **Reveal a story** — select **Hidden** on a Story module. Players can read it once its act is revealed too.

Options, adversaries, encounters, environments and events are never shown to players.
